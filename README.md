The Valentine’s Garage Management System is a truck repair and servicing management application designed to improve operations at Valentine’s Garage. The system allows trucks to be checked in for repairs while recording their condition and mileage to prevent misuse during servicing.

The application also enables mechanics to collaboratively manage repair tasks by updating completed work and adding notes. This ensures accountability and reduces the chances of repair tasks being left undone. Additionally, the garage manager, Valentine, can generate reports showing vehicle conditions, repair histories, and employee activities.

Problem Statement
Valentine’s Garage faces challenges in managing truck repairs efficiently. Some repair tasks may remain incomplete because mechanics assume another employee handled them. There is also a need to record the condition of vehicles during check-in to prevent disputes or misuse while trucks are in the garage.

This system aims to:

Improve repair task tracking
Increase employee accountability
Maintain accurate vehicle condition records
Generate management reports
